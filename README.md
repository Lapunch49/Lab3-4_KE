## Laboratory work No.4 on the subject of "Knowledge Engineering" - 
**“Development of the research prototype of the expert system”**

## Purpose and objectives of the work

**Purpose**: acquisition of skills in designing expert systems. 

**Task**: development of a prototype expert system.

**Tasks**:
1. Select a tool for the implementation of the prototype expert system based on the production model of knowledge presentation developed during laboratory work No. 3.
2. Implement the prototype of the expert system.

## Description of the subject area

A hobby is an activity that a person is happy to devote his free time to. Passion helps to distract from work and bad mood, to cope with stress. Getting carried away with something, we become happier for a while.<br>
When our brain is rebuilt from a routine or work to a hobby, it is activated in a different direction. Then it is easier for us to return to the previous lesson, to solve the problems we encountered before.<br>
When we ask someone about a hobby, we usually expect to hear the standard answer: books, embroidery, travel, movies... After all, most people really have quite traditional interests — and this is absolutely normal. But some find an outlet in rather unusual activities. Sometimes so amazing that it's hard to even imagine who invented them at all. Take our test to find out which unusual hobby might suit you.
